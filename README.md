## About

**Femboy Mod** lets you dress up. Eleven cosmetic slots that live next to your armor, dozens of clothes and accessories in any color, pride flags and shimmering glitter, and a little game around looking good: your **Drip Level** grows with every piece, full sets give bonuses, the Fashion Critic judges you mid-fight and the whole server competes at the Vibe Check Scanner.

It's SFW and meme-flavored — socks = coding skill, energy drinks, "btw" — with no third-party brands and no flashing effects.

**Languages:** English, Русский, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português (Brasil), Nederlands, Svenska, 日本語, 简体中文, Polski, Türkçe, 한국어, Italiano, Українська

## Cosmetics & Outfits

Eleven **cosmetic slots**, separate from armor, shown to everyone on the server:

| Slot | What goes there |
|---|---|
| Head | cat, fox, bunny, bear and wolf ears · cat-ear headphones · cat-ear beanie · witch hat · 10 hair clips |
| Face | heart glasses · dark shades · cyber visor · rose quartz earrings |
| Neck | UwU choker · moonstone pendant · oversized scarf |
| Top | oversized hoodie · cat-ear hoodie · crop sweater · pumpkin hoodie · ghost sheet |
| Hands | striped mittens · cat paw gloves · arm warmers · nail polish · rose quartz bracelet |
| Bottom | pleated skirt |
| Waist | belt chains · candy pail |
| Legs | programming socks · fishnet tights |
| Feet | sneakers · canvas high-tops · platform sneakers · bunny slippers (each with its own footsteps) |
| Tail | a long, fluffy fox-style tail with an S-curve that sways and reacts |
| Back | backpacks with charms |

- **Reactive ears & tail** — ears turn toward mobs they hear, flatten at a hissing creeper or when you get hurt, fold underwater and peek in at the top of the screen in first person; the tail puffs up when startled and wags after a headpat. Optional *Nya* jump sound (off by default, with a volume slider in settings)
- **Sock sliding** — wear programming socks or fishnet tights without shoes and you slide across polished floors (*Parquet Drift*); shoes, rugs and sneaking grip again
- **Winter warmth** — the cat-ear beanie and oversized scarf keep you from freezing in powder snow and give Regeneration in cold biomes
- **Every piece can be dyed** in a crafting grid — plus **19 colorways**: pride flags, glitter, pearl, neon and four exclusives you can only buy from the Wandering Cosplayer
- **Hide any piece** with the eye in its slot; it stays worn and keeps its bonus
- **Armor doesn't cover your look** — armor under your outfit is hidden (it still protects, and shows during PvP); toggle each piece auto / hide / show
- **First person too** — hoodie sleeves, mittens, arm warmers and nail polish on your hand
- **Outfit presets** — save up to five looks and switch in one click (also at the Wardrobe block)
- **Shift-click** a cosmetic in your inventory to wear it; a slot panel sits right next to the vanilla inventory
- **Lookbook** — a guide book you get on your first join: every piece by slot, where to find it, and what you have already collected

## Drip, Sets & Style Points

- **Drip Level** — every piece adds Drip, matching colors add more; a compact **Femboy Level** panel in the corner shows your tier, from *Just a Guy* to *Final Form*
- **Set bonuses** — *Full Femboy Mode* (ears + hoodie + skirt + legwear): a bit of speed, heart particles and small animals that follow you · *Crystal Clear* (rose quartz earrings + bracelet): Haste
- ✿ **Style Points** — earned for wearing sets, impressing the critic, new pieces in your collection, the rubber duck and the daily vibe check; trade them for **Style Coupons**, the currency of the Thrifter and the Cosplayer
- **Collection** — every piece you've ever worn counts (23/37…)
- **Advancements** — *Programming Socks Equipped*, *Drip: Maximum*, *Can't Decide What to Wear*, *Ready to Deploy*, *Battlestation*, *Immaculate Vibes* and more

## Backpacks & Charms

- **Canvas → Leather → Netherite** backpacks (1–3 rows) — upgrades keep everything inside; netherite doesn't burn
- Worn in the back slot, opened with **B**; they can't go inside each other (tested against dupes)
- Hang up to **three charms** on the side: shark plush, cat paw, heart pin, energy can and **pride flag badges** (11 flags, crafted from dyes) — each with a small bonus

## Gamer Room

![Gamer room](docs/images/gamer_room.png)

- **Gamer Desk & Battlestation** — **Gamer Desk** (snap a **Pink Monitor** and **Mechanical Keyboard** right onto the same desk block, or place them side by side), **Gaming PC** with toggleable RGB fans, **Ring Light**, **Desk Mic**, and **Terminal** (a crafting table that reminds you, btw, that you use Terminal)
- **Gamer Chair** — sit down and slowly heal; the better your **setup rating** (up to ★★★★★★: screen, keyboard, lights, comfort, rubber duck, posters) the faster
- **LED Strip** — four colors and a slow rainbow (never strobes) · **Moonstone Night Light** · **Neon Signs** (heart / cat / btw)
- **22 chibi plushies & Plushie Shelf** — 6 shark variants (classic, pink, gray, purple, pastel, rainbow), cat, creeper & pink creeper, enderman, dragon, axolotl, bee, frog, bunny, fox, capybara, penguin, bear, seal, red panda and rubber duck. Right-click to squish and squeak with themed particles (in the world, in mid-air, on a bed or on a shelf), hug them with both hands, wear one on your head, rest them flush on beds and rugs, or line up three on a wall-mounted **Plushie Shelf**
- **Rubber Duck** — explain your code to it: a meme answer, a bit of XP and *Insight* (faster digging)
- **20 posters** in 1×1, 1×2 and 2×2
- **Phone** — Photo Mode takes a selfie as an instant print with the mod's watermark; with paper it also prints a **Photo** you can hang in an item frame or give to a friend
- **Mirror** (and a gilded one) — see your outfit live, with everyone standing in front of it; right-click to change clothes
- **Cozy corner** — bean bag (sit and heal), fluffy rugs (square, round or heart), fairy lights, animated lava lamps, cozy cat-ear pet bed (your cats and dogs rest and heal on it), laundry basket, mini fridge (chilled Byte Energy lasts longer) and a Hollywood-bulb vanity table with a real mirror; rugs, bean bags, pet beds and lamps in all 16 dye colors
- **Plants & Seed Farming** — grow **Strawberry Bushes**, **Catnip** (4 growth stages, bone-mealable, zoomies then purring), and **Debug Flytraps** (snaps up Bugs, silverfish and endermites and grows through 3 stages) from seeds; decorate your desk with **Sakura Bonsai**, **Cat Succulent** and **Potted Grass** (right-click when the game gently suggests touching grass after two hours)

## Meme Mobs

Drip matters in a fight: the better you look, the less they hurt.

- **Bugs** swarm at night · **Caffeinated Zombies** shake with energy and hold a can · ⬛ **Hissy Cats** pounce from the dark forest · **Sock Goblins** raid laundry baskets, steal a pair of socks and slide on polished floors
- **Fashion Critic** — a rare mini-boss that reviews your outfit mid-fight. Bad drip: you're slowed. Great drip — or dark shades — leaves it speechless. Drops the exclusive wolf ears
- **Pink Creeper** — bursts into confetti and hearts, never breaks blocks · **Rubber Duck** — a tiny floating mob that helps you debug

## Servers & Addons

**Game rules** — change them in game with `/gamerule`:

| Rule | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `femboymod:style_points` | `true` | players earn Style Points |
| `femboymod:set_bonuses` | `true` | set bonuses apply |
| `femboymod:keep_cosmetics` | `false` | cosmetics stay on when you die |
| `femboymod:allow_hidden_armor` | `true` | players may hide armor under their outfit (it shows anyway while they fight another player; turn hiding off entirely on PvP servers) |
| `femboymod:cat_mischief` | `true` | cats push flower pots, candles and plushies off tables while someone watches (also needs `mobGriefing`) |
| `femboymod:drip_pvp_percent` | `0` | PvP: % more damage per Drip tier above the victim |

**Config** — `config/femboymod-common.json` (balance, mobs, furniture, style points, vibe check, friends) and `femboymod-client.json` (HUD panel, RGB animations, physics, UwU chat); in game via Mod Menu or the NeoForge mod list.

**Addon API** — a separate, documented `api` module: new cosmetics, slots, set bonuses, charms, colorways (with shimmer), effect types and conditions, renderers, chat transformers, a **player profile** for your own data (clans, ranks, points), **Style Points** and events (critic reviews, setup ratings, vibe checks, collection unlocks…). Most content is plain JSON in a data pack. Guide: [`docs/api/README.md`](docs/api/README.md), a working [example addon](example-addon).

**Compatibility** — Sodium + Iris, JEI (info pages), Mod Menu, Xaero's maps, JourneyMap. See [`docs/compat.md`](docs/compat.md).

## Installation

| | Fabric | NeoForge |
|---|---|---|
| Minecraft | 26.3 | 26.3 |
| Loader | [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) 0.19.5+ and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) | [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) 26.3.0.76+ |
| [Architectury API](https://modrinth.com/mod/architectury-api) | 22.0.4+ | 22.0.4+ |
| [GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib) | 5.5.7+ | 5.5.7+ |
| [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei), [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) | optional | optional |

1. Install the loader and the libraries above for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Download Femboy Mod from Modrinth, CurseForge or the releases.
3. Drop the `.jar` into `mods` — on **both** the client and the server.

## Development

```bash
./gradlew build                          # all modules + JUnit + server GameTests (Fabric and NeoForge)
./gradlew :fabric:runClient              # dev client (Fabric)
./gradlew :neoforge:runClient            # dev client (NeoForge)
./gradlew :fabric:runClientGameTest      # client screenshot tests
```

Architectury multi-loader: `api` / `common` / `fabric` / `neoforge` / `example-addon`, Java 25, Mojang mappings. Changes: [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
